# VETRA — Phase 13 Architecture & Technical Specification
## Government & Institutional Advanced Surveillance Integration

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Phase 13 establishes the **Government & Institutional Advanced Surveillance Integration Layer** for VETRA (Intelligent Livestock Health, Early Detection, Prevention, and Management Platform). Building upon the foundation of veterinary telemedicine, institutional reporting, and disease cluster tracking established in Phases 8 through 12, Phase 13 provides an end-to-end epidemiological surveillance architecture.

The platform bridges real-time farm-level health telemetry, computer vision observations, and predictive disease forecasts to regional, state, and institutional oversight bodies—without fabricating government connectivity, credentials, or disease outbreak statuses.

```
Farm & Animal Health Data (IoT / Edge CV / Multimodal)
```

### 2. Epidemiological Event Engine

#### 2.1 Overview & Schema
The Epidemiological Event Engine creates structured, immutable, and auditable event records that capture health anomalies across one or more farms and animals.

- **Collection**: `epidemiological_events`
- **Identifier**: `EPI-YYYY-XXXX` (deterministic year and sequential padding)
- **Supported Event Types**:
  - `unusual_mortality`: Unexpected death rates exceeding species and seasonal baselines.
  - `abnormal_health_cluster`: Correlated biometric, behavioral, or temperature anomalies.
  - `disease_suspect`: Clinical signs or predictive indicators aligning with monitored conditions.
  - `surveillance_signal`: Early anomaly generated by automated IoT or computer vision algorithms.
  - `cross_farm_signal`: Correlated signals detected across distinct geographic farm entities.
  - `geographic_cluster`: Statistically significant spatial density of alerts.
  - `persistent_health_anomaly`: Longitudinal health score deterioration not resolving within standard windows.
  - `veterinary_escalation`: Escalated directly by an attending veterinarian from a clinical case.

#### 2.2 Event Lifecycle & States
To protect epidemiological integrity and prevent panic or regulatory misattribution, VETRA strictly differentiates detection confidence levels:

1. **SIGNAL**: Automated detection based on sensor, vision, or predictive thresholds.
2. **SUSPECT**: Corroborated by veterinary case notes, lab requisitions, or multiple sensors.
3. **REVIEW_REQUIRED**: Triaged by surveillance algorithms requiring human institutional inspection.
4. **UNDER_REVIEW**: Assigned to an Institutional Officer or Veterinarian for formal investigation.
5. **CONFIRMED**: Human-verified epidemiological event with verified veterinary/institutional sign-off.
6. **DISMISSED**: Marked non-critical or false-positive with documented audit rationale.

> **CRITICAL SAFEGUARD**: VETRA **NEVER** automatically marks an event as `CONFIRMED`. Only authorized human institutional officers or administrators can confirm an event after rigorous review.

### 3. Advanced Cluster Detection & Early Warning

#### 3.1 Cluster Aggregation Engine
Building upon the existing geospatial algorithms (`backend/app/services/geospatial_service.py`), the Phase 13 Cluster Engine (`InstitutionalEarlyWarningService`) performs multi-dimensional cluster aggregation:

- **Temporal Clustering**: Groups events within sliding 7-day, 14-day, and 30-day windows.
- **Geographic Clustering**: Uses Haversine distance calculations to determine cluster boundaries, centroids, and affected radius (km).
- **Species Clustering**: Isolates species-specific transmission profiles (e.g., cattle vs. goats vs. buffalo).
- **Symptom / Signal Correlation**: Correlates biometric anomalies (pyrexia, tachypnea, reduced rumination) with clinical diagnoses.
- **Cluster Identifier**: `CL-YYYY-XXXX`

#### 3.2 Cross-Farm Early Warning Center
Institutional warnings are issued when aggregated signals exceed baseline regional thresholds:
- **Collection**: `institutional_warnings`
- **Identifier**: `WARN-YYYY-XXXX`
- **Warning Severities**: `low`, `medium`, `high`, `critical`
- **Warning Statuses**: `OPEN`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `DISMISSED`, `ESCALATED`, `CLOSED`
- **Safety Policy**: Warnings indicate statistical anomaly density; they do **NOT** autonomously declare disease outbreaks.

#### 3.3 Multi-Source Evidence Correlation Matrix
The surveillance layer constructs an explainable evidence provenance matrix incorporating:
- **IoT Telemetry**: Temperature, heart rate, respiration, activity, and rumination metrics.
- **Edge & Multimodal Computer Vision**: Body condition score (BCS), mobility/lameness score, facial/ocular discharge indicators.
- **Predictive AI**: 7-day health forecasts, disease risk vectors, and trajectory confidence scores.
- **Veterinary Cases**: Attending clinician diagnosis, case severity, and escalation logs.
- **Laboratory Records**: Registered reference IDs and status (retaining `not_available` when unperformed).
- **Preventive Care**: Herd vaccination history, deworming compliance, and treatment records.

### 4. Government Data Package Generation

#### 4.1 Packaging Philosophy
A government data package represents an **exportable, standardized epidemiological evidence dossier** intended for regulatory reporting or inter-agency coordination.

> **IMPORTANT**: A Government Data Package **IS NOT** an automatic government submission. It is a strictly controlled export format requiring human authorization. Every exported document explicitly bears the disclaimer:
> *"Government-ready export package — not an official submission."*

#### 4.2 Package Specification
- **Collection**: `government_data_packages`
- **Identifier**: `PKG-YYYY-XXXX`
- **Schema Version**: `vetra-gov-v1.0`
- **Supported Export Formats**:
  1. **JSON**: Fully structured, standardized JSON payload with SHA-256 integrity hash.
  2. **CSV**: Tabular summaries of events, affected farms, species breakdowns, and severity metrics.
  3. **PDF**: Formal multi-page institutional dossier built using ReportLab with official headers, safety disclaimers, evidence provenance tables, and signature blocks.

### 5. External Adapter Framework & 8 Safety Gates

#### 5.1 Architecture
The adapter framework extends `InstitutionalReportingAdapter` into a modular interface:
- `validate_package(package_data)`
- `submit_package(package_data, auth_context)`
- `check_status(package_id)`
- `retrieve_acknowledgement(package_id)`

#### 5.2 Mandatory Default State
- The default adapter state is strictly **`NOT_CONFIGURED`**.
- In this state, any submission attempt is immediately intercepted and blocked.
- No live government URLs, fake credentials, or simulated approvals are presented as official responses.
- When enabled in development/test environments, the mock adapter is prominently designated `SIMULATION`.

#### 5.3 The 8 Mandatory Submission Safety Gates
Before any package can be dispatched to an external adapter, it must pass all 8 gates:

```
[Gate 1: Data Validation] ──► Schema integrity, required keys, checksum
            │
[Gate 2: Required Fields] ──► Reporting period, entity ID, events summary
            │
[Gate 3: Human Review]   ──► Verified review state completed by clinician/officer
            │
[Gate 4: Human Approval] ──► Explicitly approved by Admin or Institutional Officer
            │
[Gate 5: Adapter Config] ──► Adapter status must be CONFIGURED (NOT_CONFIGURED blocks)
            │
[Gate 6: Health Check]   ──► Real-time communication and ping test passed
            │
[Gate 7: Explicit Auth]  ──► Authorized user initiates submission with active session
            │
[Gate 8: Audit Logging]  ──► Cryptographically recorded audit log entry generated
            │
            ▼
[External Submission Authorized]
```

If **any single gate** fails, the operation is rejected with an explanatory HTTP 400/403/422 status and logged in `audit_logs`.

### 6. Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Farm Tenancy

| Role | Farm Data Access | Cross-Farm Surveillance | Event Review / Confirm | Package Generation | External Submission |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Farmer** | Own farm only | ❌ Denied | ❌ Denied | ❌ Denied | ❌ Denied |
| **Veterinarian** | Assigned farms | ⚠️ Clinical Context | Can Review / Add Notes | Draft Packages | ❌ Denied |
| **Institutional Officer**| Regional herds | ✅ Full Access | ✅ Can Review & Confirm | ✅ Can Generate & Approve | ✅ Authorized Gate |
| **Admin** | System-wide | ✅ Full Access | ✅ Full Access | ✅ Full Access | ✅ Full Access |

- **Strict Isolation**: Farmers can never query or view other farms' coordinates, alert history, or health data.
- **Geographic Generalization**: Location details in public and cross-farm views are aggregated to regional or district centroids.

---

### 7. Privacy, Data Minimization & PII Protection

- **PII Masking**: Farmer names, phone numbers, and exact residential addresses are stripped from government export payloads unless required by certified statutory order.
- **Credential Protection**: Passwords, JWT secrets, database connection URIs, and internal keys are completely excluded from exports, logs, and frontend payloads.
- **Data Minimization**: Only epidemiological variables relevant to the event window (biometrics, symptoms, species, counts) are transmitted.

---

### 8. Audit Logging & Provenance

Every state transition and data access event is recorded in `audit_logs`:
- Event creation, modification, review, confirmation, and dismissal.
- Early warning issuance, acknowledgment, and closure.
- Government data package compilation, validation, approval, and submission attempts.
- Adapter configuration changes and connectivity health checks.

---

### 9. Assistive AI Boundaries (Gemini)

- Gemini integration (`ai_engine/gemini_service.py`) provides draft epidemiological narrative generation and timeline summarization.
- **Strict Constraints**:
  - All AI-generated narratives are stamped `DRAFT: AI-Assisted Narrative`.
  - Gemini **CANNOT** confirm disease events or outbreaks.
  - Gemini **CANNOT** authorize government package submission.
  - Gemini **CANNOT** fabricate laboratory findings or veterinary diagnoses.

---

### 10. Known Limitations & Operational Constraints

1. **Integration State**: Live government agency APIs (e.g., DAHD / INAPH) require certified governmental MOU and production credentials. The default state remains `NOT_CONFIGURED`.
2. **Diagnostic Authority**: VETRA is an early-warning and decision-support system; definitive pathogen isolation requires accredited laboratory verification.
