# VETRA Phase 11 — Advanced Predictive AI & Livestock Health Forecasting
## Technical Reference, Mathematical Models & Clinical Architecture

---

### 1. Executive Summary

Phase 11 extends VETRA from current health detection into **Predictive Livestock Health Intelligence**. By continuously synthesizing chronological multi-sensor IoT telemetry, visual inspections, edge inferences, clinical alerts, preventive schedules, and surveillance risk records over bounded historical horizons, the engine identifies early health deterioration trajectories, prospective risk, early warning windows, and targeted preventive triage opportunities 24 to 72 hours before acute clinical onset.

```
HISTORICAL DATA (Bounded Windows: 6h, 12h, 24h, 48h, 72h, 7d, 14d, 30d)
[IoT Telemetry] + [Visual Health] + [Edge Events] + [Alerts] + [Preventive Records] + [Surveillance]
                                      ↓
                         Feature Engineering Engine
        (Rolling means, std deviations, OLS slopes, drop rates, compliance)
                                      ↓
                             Trend Engine
       (Stable, Improving, Deteriorating, Rapid Deterioration, Volatile, Insufficient)
                                      ↓
                     PredictiveModelAdapter Hierarchy
            [Deterministic Baseline: VETRA-PredictiveModel-v1.0]
     (Pluggable stubs: Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, Temporal LSTM)
                                      ↓
                     Calibrated Predictive Risk Score (0–100)
               (Categorized: LOW, MODERATE, HIGH, CRITICAL, INSUFFICIENT)
                                      ↓
                   Confidence Engine (Calibrated 0.20 – 0.88 Max)
     (Penalizes sparse data, stale sensors; rewards multi-sensor continuity)
                                      ↓
                   Feature-Grounded Explainability & Gemini AI
                 (Primary drivers, natural language clinical narrative)
                                      ↓
                     Proactive Intervention & Escalation
           [Alerts (30m Cooldown)] | [Veterinary Case Escalation] | [Herd Surveillance]
```

### 2. Clinical Safety & Regulatory Mandate

Predictive scores generated by Phase 11 are **decision-support estimates**, not veterinary clinical diagnoses. In compliance with veterinary medical ethics and software safety standards:
1. **No Certainty Claims**: The system never asserts that an animal will definitely develop disease.
2. **Clinical Safety Language**: Uses standardized nomenclature:
   - *"Elevated predicted health risk"*
   - *"Early deterioration signal detected"*
   - *"Veterinary review may be appropriate"*
   - *"Additional monitoring recommended"*
   - *"Prediction confidence is limited"*
3. **Mandatory Provenance & Limitations**: Every prediction record includes:
   - Model name (`VETRA Predictive Baseline`)
   - Model version (`VETRA-PredictiveModel-v1.0`)
   - Feature version (`v1.0`)
   - Training status (`deterministic_baseline`)
   - Data window used (e.g. 48 hours)
   - Calibrated confidence score
   - Standard clinical safety disclaimer
4. **Physician-in-the-Loop**: Diagnostic confirmation and therapeutic prescriptions remain strictly reserved for licensed veterinarians.

### 4. Longitudinal Trend Engine (`ai_engine/trend_engine.py`)

The trend engine analyzes chronological measurements to determine trajectory direction, velocity, and consistency without overreacting to single isolated anomalies.

#### 4.1 Trajectory Classifications
1. **`stable`**: Vital parameters remain within standard healthy bounds (temp 38.0–39.3°C, HR 48–88 BPM, Resp 15–32 /m).
2. **`improving`**: Parameters recovering towards physiological baseline.
3. **`deteriorating`**: Progressive multi-reading divergence away from healthy baselines.
4. **`rapid_deterioration`**: High-velocity divergence exceeding critical rate-of-change thresholds (slope > 0.25°C/reading or multi-system simultaneous decline).
5. **`volatile`**: High variance with alternating positive and negative slopes.
6. **`insufficient_data`**: Fewer than 3 sequential readings available.

#### 4.2 Slope Calculation
Using Ordinary Least Squares linear regression over chronological indices:
$$\beta = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})^2}$$

### 5. Calibrated Risk Model: `VETRA-PredictiveModel-v1.0`

The baseline deterministic predictive model calculates a calibrated 0–100 prospective risk score:

$$R_{\text{raw}} = \sum_{k} w_k \cdot C_k$$

Where component weights $w_k$ sum to 1.00:
- Current Health Risk: $0.28$
- Temperature Trajectory: $0.22$
- Rumination & Activity Depression: $0.18$
- Respiratory & Cardio Slope: $0.16$
- Visual & Multimodal Evidence: $0.12$
- Alert Recurrence: $0.10$
- Surveillance Exposure: $0.08$
- Preventive Gaps: $0.06$

#### 5.1 Forecast Window Scaling Multipliers
- **24-Hour Horizon**: $0.95 \times$ (Near-term sensitivity)
- **48-Hour Horizon**: $1.00 \times$ (Standard baseline horizon)
- **72-Hour Horizon**: $1.10 \times$ if deteriorating (compounding divergence risk); $0.95 \times$ if stable (mean reversion)
- **Recovery Attenuation**: $0.75 \times$ if trajectory is confirmed as improving.

#### 5.2 Risk Categorization
- `0.0 – 24.9`: **LOW**
- `25.0 – 44.9`: **MODERATE**
- `45.0 – 69.9`: **HIGH**
- `70.0 – 100.0`: **CRITICAL**
- `< 3 readings`: **INSUFFICIENT_DATA**

### 6. Confidence Engine

Confidence reflects the reliability and completeness of the evidence base. The confidence engine enforces strict empirical bounds:

$$\text{Confidence} \in [0.20, 0.88]$$

**Factors:**
1. **Sample Volume Score** (up to +0.50): Proportional to reading count (capped at 30 readings).
2. **Recency Score** (up to +0.20): Fresh telemetry (< 60m: +0.20, < 180m: +0.12, stale: 0.0).
3. **Multimodal Bonus** (+0.10): Active camera / visual confirmation present.
4. **Trajectory Consistency**: +0.08 for directional momentum; -0.10 penalty for volatile noise.
5. **Upper Cap**: The confidence engine strictly caps confidence at 0.88 max, ensuring the system never outputs 95–100% confidence casually on biological signals.

### 7. Pluggable Model Architecture (`PredictiveModelAdapter`)

All predictive models implement the abstract base class:
```python
class PredictiveModelAdapter(ABC):
    @abstractmethod
    def train(self, dataset: Any) -> dict[str, Any]: pass
    @abstractmethod
    def predict(self, features: dict[str, Any], context: Optional[dict[str, Any]] = None) -> dict[str, Any]: pass
    @abstractmethod
    def evaluate(self, validation_data: Any) -> dict[str, Any]: pass
    @abstractmethod
    def explain(self, features: dict[str, Any], prediction: dict[str, Any]) -> str: pass
    @abstractmethod
    def health(self) -> dict[str, Any]: pass
```

Implemented Adapters:
1. `DeterministicPredictiveModel`: Active baseline (`VETRA-PredictiveModel-v1.0`).
2. `RandomForestPredictiveModel`: Architecture registered (`architecture_ready_unfitted`).
3. `GradientBoostingPredictiveModel`: Architecture registered (`architecture_ready_unfitted`).
4. `TemporalLSTMModel`: Architecture registered (`architecture_ready_unfitted`).

### 8. Explainability & Gemini AI Integration

Every prospective assessment generates a natural-language clinical explanation grounded exclusively in verified features:
1. **Deterministic Grounding**: Explicitly lists contributing factors (e.g. *"Temperature increased +1.20°C over baseline"*, *"Activity decreased 38.5%"*).
2. **Gemini Clinical Summarization**: Optional natural-language clinical narrative generated via Gemini API when configured.
3. **Zero-Failure Fallback**: If the Gemini API key is absent, rate-limited, or unavailable, the system transparently produces a structured deterministic clinical narrative without throwing exceptions or leaking credentials.

### 9. Alert, Surveillance & Veterinary Escalation

1. **Alert Integration**:
   - Types: `predictive_health_risk`, `predictive_deterioration`, `rapid_health_decline`, `persistent_multimodal_concern`.
   - **30-Minute Cooldown & Deduplication**: Prevents alert fatigue.
2. **Disease Surveillance Integration**:
   - Farm-level risk exposure incorporated into animal feature matrices.
   - Farm predictive health index: aggregated average predicted risk, risk category breakdown, herd risk trend.
3. **Veterinary Clinical Escalation**:
   - `POST /predictive/escalate/{assessment_id}` creates an actionable clinical case (`CASE-PRED-XXXXXX`) with populated findings, drivers, and forecast horizon.
   - Diagnostic determination remains reserved for the attending clinician.
